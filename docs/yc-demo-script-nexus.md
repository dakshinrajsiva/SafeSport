## Technical capabilities — the moat in plain English

**If a partner asks "what's hard about this," your one-breath answer:**

> **Three things.**  
> **First**, a **versioned knowledge graph** — every Indian regulation has a date stamp and supersedes another; one stale circular and the answer is wrong. We track the chain.  
> **Second**, **cross-domain reasoning** — most CA questions touch three to four regulators at once; we resolve conflicts between the Income-Tax Act, GST, FEMA, the sectoral regulator, and Companies Act in one pass.  
> **Third**, **calibrated outputs** — every claim cited to primary authority, every number editable, every conclusion tiered by confidence.  
> We're not selling answers. We're selling **traceable, partner-grade research**.
